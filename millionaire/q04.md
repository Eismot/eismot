## Q3: C / Finding and preserving information about evolving projects and their relationships

The proposal describes lost or hard-to-find project information, staff turnover, and changing relationships among people, software, hardware, and documents. Linked information was intended to grow with that environment rather than force it into one fixed hierarchy.

[Source](https://www.w3.org/History/1989/proposal.html)

## C / Simula

Stroustrup explicitly traces C++'s object-oriented concepts to Simula rather than Smalltalk. This identifies a design influence, not a single universal definition of object orientation.

[Source](https://www.stroustrup.com/bs_faq.html)
