## Q6: D / Inject a clock and use a fixed clock in the test

An injected clock lets the test choose the time explicitly. Sleeps, retries, and longer timeouts do not remove dependence on wall-clock time. Other nondeterministic inputs may still need control.

## C / Messaging, protected local state, and extreme late binding

Kay describes his own meaning of OOP in terms of messaging, local retention and protection of state-process, and extreme late binding. The attribution matters: this is his conception, not a claim that every OO language or textbook uses the same definition.

[Source](https://www.purl.org/stefan_ram/pub/doc_kay_oop_en)
