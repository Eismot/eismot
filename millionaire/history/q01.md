## B / The comedy show Monty Python's Flying Circus

The official Python FAQ says van Rossum was reading scripts from Monty Python's Flying Circus and wanted a short, distinctive, slightly mysterious name. The snake association is memorable, but it is not the naming story. Source: Python General FAQ, 'Why is it called Python?'.

[Source](https://docs.python.org/3/faq/general.html#why-is-it-called-python)
