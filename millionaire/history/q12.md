## Q11: A / No; causally unrelated events can remain unordered in a partial order

Lamport explains causal order as a partial order. A total ordering can be constructed consistently with it, but that does not turn an arbitrary ordering of concurrent events into a causal relationship. Logical timestamps are not automatically wall-clock time.

[Source](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/pubs.html#time-clocks)

## B / Byte addressing, which made character handling and word-index pointers awkward

B's cell model fit earlier word-addressed machines. On the byte-addressed PDP-11, characters were awkward and pointers represented as word indices required scaling. Ritchie added types and native-code compilation while evolving B toward C.

[Source](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html)
