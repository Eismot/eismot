## Q13: D / The operators already existed in B before the PDP-11 existed

Ritchie explicitly says ++ and -- appeared in B before the PDP-11. He discusses the PDP-7's auto-increment cells as a possible suggestion, while noting they were not used directly in implementing the operators, and points to compact translations as a likely stronger motivation. The evidence rejects the PDP-11 story without inventing a single certain replacement cause. Source: Ritchie, The Development of the C Language, 1993.

## A / Russell hand-coded eval, making the list representation executable

McCarthy describes M-expressions as the intended source notation. Representing functions as Lisp data was devised for the paper; S. R. Russell noticed that eval could serve as an interpreter and hand-coded it. That made the list form practical, and the planned switch to M-notation receded rather than being formally abandoned. Source: McCarthy, History of Lisp, 'The implementation of LISP'.

[Source](https://www-formal.stanford.edu/jmc/history/lisp/node3.html)
