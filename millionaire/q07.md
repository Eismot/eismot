## D / Atomicity

Atomicity makes the transaction an all-or-nothing unit. Durability concerns committed changes surviving failures; isolation concerns interference between concurrent transactions; consistency concerns preserving the database's rules.

[Source](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/tutorial-transactions.html)
