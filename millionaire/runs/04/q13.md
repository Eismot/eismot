## Q12: B / Prove the Echo file system's claimed fault-tolerance guarantees impossible

Lamport describes investigating Echo's claims about maintaining consistency despite failures and making progress with a working majority. He tried to prove the claims impossible, but instead discovered Paxos. The Greek parliament was the presentation metaphor, not an ancient algorithm he translated.

## A / Russell hand-coded eval, making the list representation executable

McCarthy describes M-expressions as the intended notation. S. R. Russell recognized that eval could serve as an interpreter and hand-coded it. The executable list form took hold, while the planned switch to M-notation receded rather than being formally abandoned.

[Source](https://www-formal.stanford.edu/jmc/history/lisp/node3.html)
