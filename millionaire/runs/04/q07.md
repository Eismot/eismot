## Q6: D / Atomicity

Atomicity makes the transaction an all-or-nothing unit. Durability concerns committed changes surviving failures; isolation concerns interference between concurrent transactions; consistency concerns preserving the database's rules.

[Source](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/tutorial-transactions.html)

## B / Distributing operating-system facilities across a network

Stroustrup says his initial work involved distributing operating-system facilities across a network. He sought Simula-style program organization with the efficiency and flexibility needed for systems programming.

[Source](https://www.stroustrup.com/bs_faq.html)
