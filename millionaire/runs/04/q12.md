## Q11: D / Declarations mirror expressions used to access the declared objects

Ritchie describes declaration syntax as mirroring expression syntax. For example, declaring int *pointer indicates that dereferencing pointer yields an int. Parentheses and composition make more elaborate declarations harder to read, but the unifying principle remains.

[Source](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/chist.html)

## B / Prove the Echo file system's claimed fault-tolerance guarantees impossible

Lamport describes investigating Echo's claims about maintaining consistency despite failures and making progress with a working majority. He tried to prove the claims impossible, but instead discovered Paxos. The Greek parliament was the presentation metaphor, not an ancient algorithm he translated.

[Source](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/pubs.html#paxos)
