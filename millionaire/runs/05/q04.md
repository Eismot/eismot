## D / Jacquard loom punched cards

Menabrea's account and Lovelace's notes discuss adapting Jacquard's punched-card principle to control the Analytical Engine's operations. It is a connection between textile automation and programmable operations, not evidence that the proposed engine was completed.

[Source](https://www.fourmilab.ch/babbage/sketch.html)
