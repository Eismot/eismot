## A / No; causally unrelated events can remain unordered in a partial order

Lamport explains causal order as a partial order. A total ordering can be constructed consistently with it, but that does not turn an arbitrary ordering of concurrent events into a causal relationship. Logical timestamps are not automatically wall-clock time.

[Source](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/pubs.html#time-clocks)
